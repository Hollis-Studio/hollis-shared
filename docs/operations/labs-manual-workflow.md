> **If you are looking for how blood work happens now:** Hollis sponsors a third-party biomarker panel membership for CORE and CONCIERGE members. The third party does all of it. Members share their own exported results with us by choice and with written consent, and we display them. We do not order, schedule, interpret, monitor, or notify. See [`biomarker-panel-program-sop.md`](./biomarker-panel-program-sop.md).
