# Member-Requested Referrals SOP

The referral half survives in changed form: Hollis makes referrals **only when a member asks for one**, and only as an introduction.

## 1. What a Hollis referral is and is not

**Is:** giving a member the name and contact information of an independent provider, at the member's request, so they can call and make their own appointment.

**Is not:** a clinical referral. Hollis does not assess whether a specialist is indicated, does not write a referral letter containing history and findings, does not state a reason for referral in clinical terms, does not fax anything to a practice, and does not track whether the member went.

The old referral-letter template — chief complaint, relevant history, pertinent medications, "questions for you," signed with a credential and NPI — is **removed**. Producing that document would be practicing medicine.

## 2. Procedure

1. **The member asks.** Referrals are member-initiated. Do not suggest a specialist because you think something is wrong; if you are concerned, the script is *"that's worth bringing to a doctor"* and nothing more specific.
2. **Give names, not diagnoses.** "Here are two primary care practices nearby that members have liked" is fine. "You should see an endocrinologist about your thyroid" is not.
3. **They schedule it.** Hollis does not call the practice, does not book, and does not coordinate.
4. **Sharing records requires a signed release.** If the member wants Hollis to send something to that provider, they sign a Release of Information first (see [`roi-form-template.md`](./roi-form-template.md)). Send only what the release names. Never send more because it seems helpful.
5. **Log it** in the member's record: date, that a referral was requested and provided, and the practice named. No clinical content.

## 3. Emergencies

A referral is never the answer to an emergency. Chest pain, difficulty breathing, stroke symptoms, uncontrolled bleeding, or suicidal ideation with plan or intent → 911, immediately. See [`walk-in-and-phone-sop.md`](./walk-in-and-phone-sop.md) §1.4.

## 4. Imaging

Hollis does not order imaging, does not have on-site imaging, and does not receive imaging results as a provider. If a member obtains imaging on their own and wants the report displayed in their Hollis record, that is the same member-shared-record path as blood work: written consent, then upload as a document. Hollis does not interpret it, does not review it for findings, and does not notify the member of anything in it.
